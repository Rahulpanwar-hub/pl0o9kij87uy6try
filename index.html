▦ Live Projectile Motion Derivation

1. Given Values
u = 20.0 m/s   θ = 45°   h = 0.0 m
g = 9.8 m/s²
2. Resolve Initial Velocity
uₓ = u cos θ = 20 cos 45° = 14.14 m/s
uᵧ = u sin θ = 20 sin 45° = 14.14 m/s
3. Equations of Motion
x(t) = uₓ t
y(t) = h + uᵧ t − ½gt²
vᵧ(t) = uᵧ − gt
4. Calculated Results
T = 2uᵧ/g = 2.89 s
H = uᵧ²/(2g) = 10.20 m
R = uₓ × T = 40.82 m
Ideal projectile model: air resistance is neglected and gravity is constant.
